## Bewertung
**Vorteile**
- information easier to find
- less likely to forget certain information (e.g., rationale)

**Nachteile**
- same structure can be too restrictive 
- multiple structures can be confusing
## Idee
- use of templates rather than free-form text 
- distinguishes between function, description, input (origin), output (destination), pre- and postconditions, side effects, rationale, dependencies to other requirements, . . . 
- or any subset thereof
## Bestandteile
- Anforderungs-ID(Siehe Github Issues)
	- Vorteil [Featurebranch](Feature%20Branches)-Benennung
- Funktion
- Beschreibung
- Vor-Kondition
- Nach-Kondition
- Ziel/Grund
